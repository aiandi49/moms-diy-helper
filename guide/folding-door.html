Putting up Mom's folding door

Twelve steps for hanging the white Spectrum Via folding door in a doorway 24 to 36 inches wide. Plan on about an hour, plus 15 minutes if an old door has to come down first.

Back to the guide

Have these ready

Tools

  • Small Phillips screwdriver
  • Tape measure and pencil
  • Drill with a 3/32-inch bit, or a small nail for pilot holes
  • Hacksaw or fine-tooth saw, and masking tape (only for trimming)
  • Hammer and flat screwdriver (only to take down an old door)
  • Safety glasses and a step stool
  • A helper

Step by step

The pictures are simple drawings to show each move, not photos of Mom's door. These steps follow the maker's instructions (LTL Home Products); if the sheet in the box says something different, follow the sheet.

  1. Step 1 Before you start

    Check the box

    Make sure it has the folded door, the top track, two handles with two small pins, track screws, anchor screws with washers, the magnetic latch plate and its small screws, and a packet of lubricant.

  2. Step 2 Before you start

    Measure the doorway

    Measure the width inside the frame at the top, from one side to the other. It must be 24 to 36 inches. Measure the height from the floor to the top of the opening on both sides and keep the smaller number.

  3. Step 3 Old door

    Take down the old door

    Skip this if the opening has no door. Have a helper hold the door by the knob. Tap each hinge pin up and out with a screwdriver and hammer, starting with the bottom hinge and ending with the top. Lift the door away.

  4. Step 4 Old door

    Remove the hinge plates

    Unscrew the hinge plates left on the frame so the folding door can sit flat against that side. Keep the door, pins, plates and screws together in case Mom ever wants it back.

  5. Step 5 Folding door

    Trim, only if needed

    If the opening is shorter than about 80 inches, tape the folded panels together and saw the extra off the bottom, the end without holes. Then cut the top track 1/8 inch shorter than the width you measured.

  6. Step 6 Folding door

    Fit the top track

    Hold the track centered against the underside of the top of the frame. Make a pilot hole through the hole nearest the middle with a small nail or a 3/32-inch drill bit, and put in one track screw. Leave it loose so the track can swing.

  7. Step 7 Folding door

    Put on the handles

    Tap the two pins into one handle, push the pins through the holes in the door's edge, and press the second handle onto them from the other side. Tap around the pins, never on the middle of the handle.

  8. Step 8 Folding door

    Hang the door

    Put a little lubricant on each roller at the top of the door. Swing one end of the track down out of the opening, choose which side the door should open from, and slide every roller onto the track. Push the door toward the middle.

  9. Step 9 Folding door

    Screw the track tight

    Swing the track back into place. Make a pilot hole at each remaining track hole, then drive in every screw, straight and snug, so the door glides smoothly.

  10. Step 10 Folding door

    Fix the anchor edge

    Slide the whole door to the side where the old hinges were. Let it hang straight up and down, then put the anchor screws through the washers and the three holes in that edge and into the frame.

  11. Step 11 Folding door

    Fit the latch plate

    Close the door until its edge meets the other side of the frame. Mark where the magnetic latch plate lines up with the metal strip on the door edge, make pilot holes, and screw the plate on with the small flathead screws.

  12. Step 12 Folding door

    Test it

    Open and close it a few times using the handle. It should glide without catching and click shut on the magnet. If it drags, check that every track screw is straight and tight.

Safety Always open and close the door by its handle. Pushing on the end of a folding door can pinch fingers against the frame. Wear safety glasses when sawing or drilling overhead, and keep the step stool on a flat floor.
Good to know A folding door closes with a magnet and doesn't lock. A small hook-and-eye latch on the inside adds privacy. The old hinge holes can be filled with wood filler and touched up with white paint later.

Guidance only. If the frame is loose or damaged, have a handyman look before hanging the door.
